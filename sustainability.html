Our Commitment to the Planet

We believe that industrial advancement should not come at the cost of the environment.

100% Recycled Yarns

By 2030, we aim to transition 80% of our core product lines to utilize certified recycled polymers, significantly reducing ocean waste.

Waterless Dyeing

Traditional textile dyeing consumes massive amounts of water. We are adopting supercritical CO2 dyeing technologies to eliminate water waste.

Solar-Powered Facilities

Our main manufacturing hubs are currently transitioning to solar energy, reducing our grid dependency by 40% over the last three years.
